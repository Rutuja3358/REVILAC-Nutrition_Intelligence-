# Quick start — REVILAC Nutrition Intelligence

1. Extract the complete ZIP into a new folder.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser for a local UI preview. Some browser speech/notification APIs require HTTPS or localhost.
3. Tap **Daily Wellness** to open the added patient wellness companion.
4. Try food choices, **Other options**, **Classify my selection**, the habit checklist, and read-aloud.
5. For full existing features, follow `README.md` and `FREE_SETUP_AND_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md`.

## Do not treat this preview as a clinical product
The new module's food cards classify broad possible nutrient contributions only. They do not provide measured nutrient quantities, individualized dietary targets, disease treatment, or a diagnosis. Do not enter real patient data in this local prototype. Browser storage may be readable on the device and does not sync. Reliable background notifications, clinical validation, secure accounts/backend, and production review remain separate tasks.
