# REVILAC Nutrition Intelligence — v3.23 Patient-first Nutrition Depth

## Added
- Added a Quick Food Alternatives flow in Nutrition Guidance for lower-cost choices, protein-containing foods, fibre, dairy-free ideas, quick meals, and familiar meal variety.
- Includes vegetarian, vegan, eggetarian and mixed preference filters; budget-aware filtering; Marathi/Hindi/English explanations.
- The flow is synchronous/local and returns immediately without an API.
- If an allergy or prescribed restriction is entered, the tool pauses suggestions rather than attempting unreliable keyword-based safety clearance.
- Uses general food-education examples only; no fabricated nutrient values, calories, clinical diagnosis or disease-specific prescription.

## Limits
- Food alternatives are starter examples, not a complete food database or a guarantee of allergy safety.
- Clinical, allergy, nutrient-data and production validation remain separate release gates.
