# REVILAC Nutrition Intelligence — Final Scope Lock

## Product identity
Independent project: **REVILAC Nutrition Intelligence**. Do not merge it with the older REVILAC Healthcare project. Do not rename it to iMAX PLUS.

## Product principle
REVILAC explains nutrition needs, nutrient functions, food-source information, and the estimated nutritional result of foods the patient chooses. The patient retains the food decision. Do not force a named food, recipe, or meal plan. Any optional examples must remain clearly optional and patient-controlled.

## Final required feature groups
1. Patient profile and relevant context.
2. Comprehensive nutrient model: energy, protein, carbohydrate, fats, fibre, sugars, vitamins, minerals, water and other relevant nutrients supported by reliable data.
3. Individualized reference needs, with applicable age/health-context safeguards and source citations.
4. Patient-controlled food-source information and comparisons, not prescriptive recipes.
5. Food intake recording by item and quantity, with recalculation after changes.
6. Daily total, estimated remaining need, trends and nutrient-gap flags with uncertainty labels.
7. Nutrition-report reading/OCR only as a support feature; verify extracted values against the original.
8. Patient timeline and relevant context correlation without asserting causation from correlation.
9. Allergy, dietary restriction, and higher-risk-context safety gates.
10. Daily wellness: hydration (respecting medical fluid limits), sleep, activity/exercise/yoga, mood, meals and follow-up reminders.
11. Multilingual, accessible, voice/picture-assisted interaction for low-literacy users.
12. Consent, privacy, backup/recovery, secure account/data handling, and cross-device support.
13. Automated tests, target-device end-to-end testing, clear release evidence, one consolidated package.
14. Final status must classify each feature as COMPLETE / PARTIAL / NOT IMPLEMENTED. Never claim 100% readiness without evidence.

## Non-negotiable safety
- Do not diagnose or claim a nutrient deficiency solely from an incomplete log or one lab result.
- Do not treat unknown nutrient values as zero.
- Do not invent nutrient values, clinical targets, food composition sources, or external integrations.
- For low blood glucose or other urgent conditions, follow appropriate safety guidance and escalation; a food-choice interface must not delay urgent care.
- This package is a prototype until clinical, privacy/security and real-device validation are documented.
