REVILAC — Nutrition Intelligence

Understand your health information. Keep the context. Make the next conversation clearer.

Our vision

One person. One evolving health story. REVILAC aims to help people organize health reports and everyday nutrition context over time, understand unfamiliar terms in plain language, notice what is missing or uncertain, and prepare better questions for a qualified health professional.

It is designed as a bridge between raw health information and informed human conversations—not as an automated doctor, diagnostic authority, or replacement for a registered dietitian or clinician.

What REVILAC is built to do

  • Keep report entries and measurements organized as a timeline.
  • Show user-entered values and reference ranges with data-quality checks.
  • Offer plain-language explanations for selected common health and nutrition terms.
  • Compare matching measurements over time while preserving units and context.
  • Support food-source choices, daily wellness habits, and follow-up reminders.
  • Export user-controlled backups and structured review records.
  • Provide a starter terminology workflow that distinguishes candidate matches from verified codes.

What REVILAC does not claim

  • It does not diagnose a disease or infer a diagnosis from one lab value.
  • It does not prescribe therapeutic diets, medicines, or supplements.
  • It does not guarantee OCR accuracy or allergy safety.
  • It does not contain every official medical code or a complete food-composition database.
  • Local browser storage is not encrypted clinical-record storage.
  • Browser reminders are not guaranteed while the app is closed.
  • The included account server is a prototype, not production cloud infrastructure.

How the workflow should work

01 · CAPTURE

Record the source

Enter a report or extract text from a file. Keep the original report available for comparison.

02 · CHECK

Verify the details

Confirm the test name, value, unit, date, reference interval and patient context. Correct OCR mistakes before saving.

03 · EXPLAIN

Understand uncertainty

Read general explanations, see missing context, and distinguish recorded information from verified interpretation.

04 · REVIEW

Prepare the next step

Compare prior entries, record questions and follow-ups, and take clinical decisions with a qualified professional.

Design principles

Evidence before confidence

Keep source, date, units and verification status visible. Unknown information must remain unknown rather than being guessed.

Human review for high risk

Pregnancy, children, serious symptoms, allergies, medicines, kidney/liver concerns and undernutrition require qualified review before personalized advice.

Understandable by default

Prefer short steps, familiar words, accessible controls and language choices. A translation must not change clinical meaning.

Privacy and user control

Use synthetic data during testing. Explain storage and export behavior, and never expose API secrets or sensitive records in screenshots or source control.

Interoperability without pretending

Terminology standards are versioned and may be licensed. Candidate mappings need source, version, context and human review.

Free-first, honest limits

Local features should remain useful without paid APIs where possible. External services are labeled as optional and not silently simulated.

Current readiness

Development prototype — not production-ready. The source package has automated checks, but real-device usability, representative OCR accuracy, security/privacy review, clinical validation, and licensed nutrition-data verification remain open.

Before real health data: deploy behind HTTPS; use a managed database, secure authentication and recovery, access controls, rate limits, encrypted backups, retention/deletion procedures, monitoring, and an independently reviewed privacy/security design. Obtain qualified clinical/nutrition review and test with a representative, consented benchmark.

REVILAC can help organize information and prepare questions. It must not delay urgent care. Seek emergency help for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.

REVILAC — Nutrition Intelligence

Enter values from a food label or a trusted food-composition source. REVILAC calculates the selected amount proportionally and sums the foods you add today. It does not invent missing nutrient values.

Important safety note

This is a tracking calculator, not a diagnosis or personalized medical diet prescription. Nutrient targets are not automatically assigned. Children, pregnancy/lactation, eating disorders, kidney/liver disease, diabetes requiring medication, severe allergies or other clinical conditions need an appropriate clinician/dietitian. Check units and source before relying on results.

1. Add or choose a food

Online food-composition lookup

Search USDA FoodData Central for a specific food item. This is a data lookup, not a food recommendation. Verify the food form, edible portion, units and source before adding it. Branded-food records may be per serving and will not be auto-filled as per-100 g data.

Nutrient values per 100 g edible portion (leave unknown values blank; use the units shown)

2. Today's selected foods

Calculated total

Totals include only numeric values entered for each food. Blank nutrient fields are excluded, not treated as zero; each total shows how many foods supplied a value. Values are estimates based on your entries, not independently verified measurements.

3. Compare with a target you enter

Enter a target only when you have a reliable reference for this person and context (for example, a clinician or qualified dietitian). REVILAC does not automatically prescribe targets. Blank target means “not set”.

The difference is arithmetic only, not a diagnosis. Needs vary by age, life stage and health context. Do not use general targets for clinical decisions without qualified review.

4. Food swap / alternative

To compare alternatives, add the alternative as a separate food with its own source and portion, then remove the food it replaces from today's entries. The totals update automatically.

This calculator does not declare foods nutritionally equivalent, decide a medically appropriate replacement, or automatically calculate a person's remaining daily requirement because those require verified nutrient targets and patient-specific review.
